## Surface

Clay Pot (மண்பாண்டம்): the church's writings column. Index at /grow/clay-pot, one page per article at /grow/clay-pot/<slug>. Mode: Read. Nav: Grow → Clay Pot. Presented as an active column; new writings are one entry in src/data/clayPot.ts.

Audience: members and Tamil readers returning for pastoral letters; English-first visitors who need to see what each piece is and which language it is in. Content: three archived writings (2016 English, Ajith Samuel; 2019 and 2020 Tamil, Rev. Jagan Sathiya Seelan), with original footnotes. Tamil kept as written, with no translation and no invented summaries. Excerpts are the writing's own opening lines.

Unresolved: the pastor should confirm the Tamil nameplate "மண்பாண்டம்" (taken from 2 Cor 4:7, Tamil O.V.).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Clay Pot is the church's own monthly. It refuses the blog-card grid and the generic "Articles" list. A nameplate, a lead letter and numbered issues make a small archive feel like a living publication.

OWN-WORLD: CTC tokens unchanged. A cream nameplate band carries மண்பாண்டம் large in Noto Serif Tamil, with CLAY POT tracked mono gold beneath and a dateline rule (Christ Tamil Church · Since 2016 · Tamil & English). Issue numerals ("No. 03") are set in Fraunces light on hairline rows. Language badges are pills.

STORY: The reader learns that the church writes, what the newest letter is and who wrote it, then picks an issue. Article pages read calmly and end with notes, previous/next issue, and a way to reach the church.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The nameplate band spans the width, with the Tamil name at display scale left and a short English standfirst and dateline right. Below it is the lead feature: No. 03, the title at h2 display scale, the byline and date, the first lines of the letter, and a bronze "Read this letter" pill. A column note sits beside it.

FORM: Tamil Christian monthly magazine with the pastor's letter, #4 on the ordered list of seven, seed key dcd9fea4.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
